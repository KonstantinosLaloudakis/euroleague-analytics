Regularized Adjusted Plus-Minus

How much does a player actually help their team win, independent of who they play with? RAPM answers this by analyzing every lineup combination across 11,000+ game segments, using machine learning to separate each player's true contribution from their teammates' and opponents' strength.

What do the columns mean?
RAPM — Overall impact: points per 100 possessions the team gains/loses with this player on court. +2.0 means the team scores 2 more points per 100 possessions than it concedes.
O-RAPM — Offensive impact only: how much better the team scores with this player on court.
D-RAPM — Defensive impact: how much the team limits opponents. Positive = good defender, Negative = defensive liability.
Prior — The box-score expectation used as a starting point. Players with limited minutes are pulled toward this value rather than zero.
Basic — RAPM without the box-score prior. Comparing Basic vs RAPM reveals which players the prior adjusts most.
Boost badge — The colored badge next to a player's name shows how much the informed prior shifted their rating vs. basic RAPM. A green boost means box-score stats suggest they're better than raw lineups show (often good players on weak teams).
